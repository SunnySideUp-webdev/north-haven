## What to review

- Does the lavender and blush balance resemble the references without feeling
  copied?
- Does the title typography feel elegant rather than childish?
- Can the page be used for several hours without feeling visually heavy?
- Are tables and controls clear enough despite the quiet palette?
- Does Today remain calmer than Insights and Work?
- Does the Report Builder feel like part of Insights rather than a separate
  product?
- Does the exported report preserve North Haven's identity without looking like
  an application screenshot?

These designs establish the complete desktop visual language, including the
critical empty, loading, validation, Calendar, OOO, capacity, confirmation, and
End-of-Day completion states. Final copy and exact spacing will be translated
into implementation tokens after approval. Behavior details live in
[`docs/11-interaction-states.md`](../../docs/11-interaction-states.md).
Reporting behavior and privacy rules live in
[`docs/12-reporting-and-exports.md`](../../docs/12-reporting-and-exports.md).

All interface typography, including the North Haven wordmark and page titles,
uses DM Sans with no decorative italic treatment.
